---
title: 'I Always Wanna Die (Sometimes)'
date: '2026-06-24'
slug: 'i-always-wanna-die-sometimes'
excerpt: 'Some nights feel endless, but sometimes staying a little longer is enough to see if hope is still there.'
category: 'poems'
---

Some nights feel endless,<br />
Like the dark won't let me go.<br />
I carry storms inside my chest,<br />
That nobody seems to know.

But morning knocks softly,<br />
And the sky begins to heal.<br />
I stay a little longer,<br />
To see if hope is real.

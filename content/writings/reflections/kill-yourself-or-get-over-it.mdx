Life is unfair. It doesn’t care about plans, efforts, or how much you try to be ready. Sometimes it knocks you down for no reason, and it can feel like the world is stacked against you. But the only choice we have is to keep moving forward.

Every challenge, every failure, every setback is part of the journey, a reminder that growth doesn’t come easy. Life doesn’t owe us fairness, comfort, or certainty. What it gives is the chance to stand back up, to face what comes next, and to keep going even when the path is rough. Surviving the unfairness isn’t weakness; it’s the quiet strength that shapes us.

So stop waiting for life to be fair, push through, adapt, and keep moving. Get over it, and keep going.

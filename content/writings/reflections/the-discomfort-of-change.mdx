Your brain resists change.

In psychology, this is often explained through ***homeostasis***, the tendency of the body and mind to maintain stability, even when your current state isn’t ideal. So when you try to change habits, your system pushes back. It feels uncomfortable, unfamiliar, even wrong.

There’s also the concept of ***neuroplasticity***. Your brain is constantly rewiring itself when you learn new behaviors or build new habits. That process takes time, repetition, and discomfort in the early stages.

But here’s the important part:

Feeling “weird” doesn’t always mean something is wrong.

Sometimes weird means growth.
Sometimes weird means misalignment.

The difference comes down to reflection:

Is this aligned with the person I want to become?
Is this discomfort coming from discipline, or from forcing something unnatural?
Is it improving me over time, even slowly?

If yes, push through the discomfort.
If not, adjust, don’t force it.

Because growth and misalignment can feel surprisingly similar at the start.

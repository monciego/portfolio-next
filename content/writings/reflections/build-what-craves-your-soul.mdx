Build what craves your soul.

Not what looks impressive on paper.
Not what others expect from you.
Not what trends tell you to chase.

But the things that keep returning to your mind when everything is quiet.

The ideas you can’t fully ignore.
The projects you keep thinking about even when you’re tired.
The work that feels like it’s asking to exist through you.

Because creation isn’t just output.

It’s alignment.

Change feels strange. When you start letting go of old habits, bad patterns, and the things that used to define your routine, it can feel like your whole body and life are resisting. That’s because they are, your system isn’t used to this new version of you yet.

So if it feels uncomfortable, unfamiliar, even a little chaotic... **keep going**. **It gets better.**

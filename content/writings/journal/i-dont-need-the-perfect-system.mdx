Today I’m organizing all my notes and ideas.

They’re scattered across different apps, and everything feels disorganized. While going through them, I keep discovering old thoughts, unfinished projects, ideas I once believed in, things that never came to life.

And I noticed a pattern in myself.

I keep trying to find the “right” way to organize everything first. I watch videos, read setups, try to figure out the perfect system… and I end up not really doing the actual work.

So I decided I’ll just do it my own way.

I’ll build my own structure while I organize things. Then I’ll just adjust it if I learn something better or if something actually fits what I need. No overthinking the system anymore.

After I finish organizing, I’ll go back to the ideas and continue them.

Not just store them this time.
